有問題歡迎來信 zoey.meala@gmail.com，通常一到兩個工作天回覆。

對開在做什麼？

選幾張自己的照片，對開會用十二種設計排成桌布：孔版、藍曬、旅行海報、底片、點陣、拍貼、收藏卡、雜誌封面、大字海報、長虹玻璃、色帶、貼圖。在 iPhone Duo 上，闔上時外螢幕顯示主角，打開才看到全部；在其他 iPhone 上是一張直式桌布。

怎麼設成桌布？

按「存到相簿」會存兩張：清楚的那張設成鎖定畫面，模糊的那張設成主畫面。到 iPhone「設定」→「背景圖片」→「加入新的背景圖片」，選照片即可。iPhone Duo 闔上時，外螢幕會自動顯示正中間那一條。

照片會上傳嗎？

不會。排版、去背、出圖全部在你的手機上完成，沒有帳號，也沒有我們自己的伺服器。

免費版和 Pro 差在哪？

免費版可以使用大字海報、底片、色帶三款，存出的桌布沒有浮水印。Pro 是一次買斷，解鎖全部款式與配色，之後新增的款式也包含在內。不是訂閱，不會自動扣款。

換手機或重裝後怎麼拿回 Pro？

用同一個 Apple 帳號登入 App Store，到對開的「設定」或付費頁按「還原購買」。

主角被裁到了

在款式頁下方的「主角」換一張照片，或選主體在畫面中間的照片。直式、主體清楚的照片效果最好。

退款

購買由 Apple 處理，我們無法直接退款。請到 reportaproblem.apple.com 申請。

Gatefold — Support

Questions? Email zoey.meala@gmail.com

What does Gatefold do?

Pick a few of your own photos and Gatefold lays them out as wallpapers in twelve designs: riso, cyanotype, travel poster, film strip, 1-bit, photo booth, trading card, magazine, big type, fluted glass, color bands and stickers. On iPhone Duo the cover screen shows the hero when closed and the whole design when open. On other iPhones you get a portrait wallpaper.

How do I set the wallpaper?

Save to Photos stores two images. Use the sharp one for your Lock Screen and the blurred one for your Home Screen: Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper. On iPhone Duo the cover screen shows the middle strip automatically.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Layout, subject cut-outs and rendering all happen on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no servers of our own.

Free vs Pro

The free version includes Big type, Film strip and Color bands, with no watermark. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks every style and color, including styles added later. It is not a subscription.

Restoring Pro

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account and tap Restore purchase in Settings or on the Pro screen.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
