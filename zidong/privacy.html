隱私政策

字動 · 最後更新：2026 年 11 月

一句話版本

本 App 不收集、不上傳、不分享任何個人資料。沒有帳號、沒有廣告、沒有第三方分析工具。照片、影片與你打的字永遠不會離開手機。
App 只會送出匿名的使用統計（例如「用了哪個範本、字效」「輸出了哪種格式」），用來改進 App，詳見下方「匿名使用統計」。

我們不收集什麼

  • 不需要註冊，也沒有登入功能
  • 不收集姓名、電子郵件、電話或任何聯絡資訊
  • 不收集位置或通訊錄
  • 不使用第三方分析或廣告 SDK，App 內沒有任何廣告
  • 不使用廣告識別碼（IDFA），不跨 App 或網站追蹤你
  • 不追蹤你，也不會把資料提供給其他公司

匿名使用統計

為了知道哪些功能好用、哪裡讓人卡住，App 會送出匿名事件到我們自己的伺服器（Cloudflare）：

  • App 第一次開啟時隨機產生的編號（由 App 自己產生，不是廣告識別碼，也跟你的 Apple 帳號無關；刪除 App 就會消失）
  • App 版本、系統語言、地區設定、手機型號
  • 用了哪些功能與次數，例如開啟 App、選「店家／自己用」與行業類別、打開哪個範本、換到哪個字效、加了哪種貼紙、放的是照片還是影片（只記種類與大約秒數）、輸出了哪種格式與比例、有沒有浮水印、行事曆提醒的頻率（每天／每週／每月）、看到付費頁、點了哪個方案、購買是否完成、恢復購買，以及輸出失敗等錯誤代碼

不會送出：照片、影片、你打的任何文字（主標、副標、店名、@帳號、紀念日名字都不會）、檔名、logo、姓名、Apple 帳號、付款資料或位置。伺服器不記錄 IP 位址，事件資料保存 180 天後自動刪除，只用於改進本 App，不提供給任何第三方，也不會用於廣告或追蹤。

照片與影片

你用系統的照片選擇器挑的照片或影片，才會被複製進 App 當背景；App 不會讀取或掃描你的相簿。按下儲存時，才把作品寫進「照片」App（只要求「加入照片」權限）。

在裝置上處理

字效動畫、照片與影片背景、輸出影片全部在你的手機上運算。少數範本會用 Apple 內建的 Vision 框架在裝置上辨識照片主角，結果不會離開手機。照片、影片與你打的字都不會上傳，我們也看不到。

通知

行事曆的固定貼文提醒、檔期與紀念日提醒，都是在你的手機上排程的本機通知，不經過任何伺服器。通知權限是選用的。

存在裝置上的資料

作品、匯入的素材、品牌套組（店名、@帳號、logo、品牌色）、行事曆提醒與偏好設定，只存在 App 的沙盒內。刪除 App 就會一併刪除。若你開啟了 iCloud 或電腦備份，iOS 備份可能依你自己的設定包含這些 App 資料。

付費與訂閱

字動 Pro 提供月訂、年訂與一次買斷，全部由 Apple App Store 處理付款，我們不會取得你的姓名、信用卡或付款資料，只會從 Apple 收到「是否已購買」的驗證結果。月訂與年訂為自動續訂，可隨時在 iPhone「設定」→ 你的名字 →「訂閱」中取消。

兒童

本 App 分級為 4+。匿名使用統計不含任何可識別個人的資料，因此也不會收集兒童的個人資料。

政策變更

若日後有變更，會更新本頁並修改上方的更新日期。

Privacy Policy

Zidong (字動) · Last updated: November 2026

In short

This app does not collect, upload or share any personal data. No account, no ads, no third-party analytics. Your photos, videos and the text you type never leave your phone. The app only sends anonymous usage statistics (such as which template or effect was used, or which export format was chosen) to help improve it — see “Anonymous usage statistics” below.

What we do not collect

  • No sign-up and no login
  • No name, email, phone number or any contact information
  • No location and no contacts
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and no ads inside the app
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) and no tracking across apps or websites
  • We do not track you and we do not share data with anyone

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features work and where people get stuck, the app sends anonymous events to our own server (Cloudflare):

  • A random ID generated by the app when it is first opened (not an advertising ID and not linked to your Apple Account; deleting the app removes it)
  • App version, system language, region setting and device model
  • Which features were used and how often, such as opening the app, choosing “shop / personal” and an industry category, which template was opened, which text effect was picked, which kind of sticker was added, whether a photo or a video was used (kind and rough length only), which export format and aspect ratio were used and whether it had a watermark, how often a calendar reminder repeats (daily / weekly / monthly), seeing the Pro page, which plan was tapped, whether a purchase completed, restoring purchases, and error codes such as a failed export

Never sent: photos, videos, any text you type (headlines, subtitles, shop name, @handle or anniversary names), file names, logos, your name, Apple Account, payment details or location. The server does not log IP addresses, events are deleted automatically after 180 days, and they are used only to improve this app — never shared with third parties and never used for advertising or tracking.

Photos and videos

Only the photos or videos you pick with the system photo picker are copied into the app as backgrounds; the app never reads or scans your photo library. Your work is written to Photos only when you tap save (add-only permission).

On-device processing

Text animations, photo and video backgrounds and video export all run on your device. A few templates use Apple's built-in Vision framework on device to find the main subject of a photo; results never leave the phone. Your photos, videos and text are never uploaded and we cannot see them.

Notifications

Reminders for recurring posts, seasonal dates and anniversaries are local notifications scheduled on your phone; no server is involved. Notification permission is optional.

Data kept on your device

Your works, imported media, brand kit (shop name, @handle, logo, colours), reminders and preferences live only in the app's sandbox. Deleting the app deletes them. If you use iCloud or computer backups, iOS backups may include this app data according to your own settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Zidong Pro is offered as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, all processed by the Apple App Store. We never receive your name, card or payment details — only Apple's verification of whether Pro has been purchased. Subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled any time in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Children

This app is rated 4+. The anonymous usage statistics contain nothing that identifies a person, so no personal data is collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
