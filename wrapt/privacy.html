隱私政策

繞著你 · 最後更新：2026 年 10 月

一句話版本

本 App 不收集、不上傳、不分享任何個人資料。沒有帳號、沒有廣告。照片與你打的字永遠不會離開手機。
從 1.1 版起，App 會送出匿名的使用統計（例如「選了哪個繞法」「有沒有存檔」），用來改進 App，詳見下方。

我們不收集什麼

  • 不需要註冊，也沒有登入功能
  • 不收集姓名、電子郵件、電話或任何聯絡資訊
  • 不收集位置或通訊錄
  • 不使用第三方分析或廣告 SDK，App 內沒有任何廣告
  • 不使用廣告識別碼（IDFA），不跨 App 或網站追蹤你
  • 不追蹤你，也不會把資料提供給其他公司

相簿

只讀取你選擇的照片與影片；按下儲存時才把作品寫回相簿。不會掃描整個相簿。

照片與影片的處理

辨識主角輪廓（人物、寵物、食物等）使用 Apple 內建的 Vision 框架，完全在裝置上運算。照片、影片與分析結果都不會離開你的手機，我們也看不到。

字體下載

部分字體由 Apple 的字型服務提供下載，這是 iOS 系統功能；下載時不會附帶你的照片、文字或任何個人資料。

匿名使用統計（1.1 版起）

為了知道哪些功能好用、哪裡讓人卡住，App 會送出匿名事件到我們自己的伺服器（Cloudflare）：

  • App 第一次開啟時隨機產生的編號（不是廣告識別碼，也跟你的 Apple 帳號無關；刪除 App 就會消失）
  • App 版本、系統語言、地區設定、手機型號
  • 操作事件與數量，例如開啟 App、載入照片（只記「是照片或影片、有沒有找到主角」）、選了哪個繞法／風格／字體／圖案、打了幾個字（只有字數，不含內容）、打開輸出、存檔或分享、看到付費頁、選了哪個方案、購買是否完成

不會送出：照片、影片、你打的文字、姓名、Apple 帳號或付款資料。伺服器不記錄 IP 位址，事件資料保存 180 天後自動刪除，只用於改進本 App，不提供給任何第三方。

存在裝置上的資料

你輸入的文字、偏好設定與自訂風格存在 App 的沙盒內。刪除 App 就會一併刪除。

付費與訂閱

繞著你 Pro 提供一次買斷、月訂與季訂（每 3 個月）三種方案，全部由 Apple App Store 處理付款，我們不會取得你的姓名、信用卡或付款資料，只會從 Apple 收到「此裝置是否已購買」的驗證結果。月訂與季訂為自動續訂，可隨時在 iPhone「設定」→ 你的名字 →「訂閱」中取消。

兒童

本 App 分級為 4+。匿名使用統計不含任何可識別個人的資料，因此也不會收集兒童的個人資料。

政策變更

若日後有變更，會更新本頁並修改上方的更新日期。

Privacy Policy

Wrapt · Last updated: October 2026

In short

This app does not collect, upload or share any personal data. No account, no ads. Your photos and the text you type never leave your phone. Starting with version 1.1, the app sends anonymous usage statistics (such as which wrap style was chosen or whether a work was saved) to help improve it — see below.

What we do not collect

  • No sign-up and no login
  • No name, email, phone number or any contact information
  • No location and no contacts
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and no ads inside the app
  • We do not track you and we do not share data with anyone
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) and no tracking across apps or websites

Anonymous usage statistics (from version 1.1)

To learn which features work and where people get stuck, the app sends anonymous events to our own server (Cloudflare):

  • A random ID generated when the app is first opened (not an advertising ID and not linked to your Apple Account; deleting the app removes it)
  • App version, system language, region setting and device model
  • Actions and counts, such as opening the app, loading a photo (only whether it is a photo or video and whether a subject was found), which wrap, style, font or pattern was chosen, how many characters were typed (count only, never the text), opening export, saving or sharing, seeing the Pro page, which plan was tapped and whether a purchase completed

Never sent: photos, videos, the text you type, your name, Apple Account or payment details. The server does not log IP addresses, events are deleted automatically after 180 days, and they are used only to improve this app and never shared with third parties.

Photo library

The app reads only the photos and videos you pick, and writes your work back to your library only when you tap save. It never scans the rest of your library.

Photo and video processing

Finding the subject (people, pets, food and more) uses Apple's built-in Vision framework and runs entirely on your device. Your photos, videos and analysis results never leave your phone and we cannot access them.

Font downloads

Some fonts are downloaded from Apple's font service, an iOS system feature. No photos, text or personal data are sent with that request.

Data kept on your device

Your text, preferences and saved styles live in the app's sandbox. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases and subscriptions

Wrapt Pro is offered as a one-time purchase, a monthly subscription or a 3-month subscription, all processed by the Apple App Store. We never receive your name, card or payment details — only Apple's verification of whether Pro has been purchased. Subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled any time in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Children

This app is rated 4+. The anonymous usage statistics contain nothing that identifies a person, so no personal data is collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
